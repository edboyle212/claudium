# Sitting 00 - what claude already knows

**Who:** You  |  **About:** 10 min

Start a **new chat** in Claude. Attach nothing. Paste everything below the line.

---

I have been using you for a while to work on my business. I am about to build
a set of files that hold what you need to know about it, and I want to see
what you are already holding first.

## What to do

Go through what you know about my business - what I have asked you to
remember, and, if you can search our past conversations, what comes up there
too. List it back to me.

Group it under these headings, and skip any you have nothing for:

- What the business is and who it serves
- What I sell, and what it costs
- Customers, clients, partners and suppliers
- How I sound - voice, tone, words I like or avoid
- Pricing, offers and discounts
- Tools I use and how I work
- Decisions I have made
- Anything else

## How to write it

One short line per item. Plain facts, no commentary. Mark each line:

- **(told)** - I said this to you directly
- **(worked out)** - you inferred it rather than being told
- **(old)** - it came up more than about three months ago
- **(unsure)** - you cannot tell which

Guessing at the source is worse than admitting you cannot tell.

## What not to do

Do not fill gaps. If you know six things, list six. Do not round it out to
something that reads complete.

Do not tidy up contradictions. If two things you remember disagree, list both
and say so. That is exactly what I need to see.

Do not write it as a summary or a profile. A flat list is what I want.

## At the end

Tell me which two or three items you are least confident about, and why. Then
tell me which of these things, if you forgot them tomorrow, would cost me the
most to reconstruct.
