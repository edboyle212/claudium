# Sitting 01 - compare what you each know

**Who:** Everyone together  |  **About:** 10 min

Start a **new chat** in Claude. Attach nothing. Paste everything below the line.

---

Two or more people have each asked you what you already knew about our
business. We have the lists in front of us. Compare them.

I will paste them one after another. Wait until I say they are all in before
you say anything.

## What to do

1. **Agreements.** What appears on every list, said the same way? List these
   briefly - they are probably true and they are the easy part.
2. **Contradictions.** Where do the lists disagree about the same thing - a
   price, a policy, a decision, how we describe ourselves? List each one
   with both versions side by side. Do not guess which is right.
3. **Held by one person only.** What appears on one list and no other? For
   each, say one line on what it would cost us if that person were away and
   nobody else knew it.
4. **Suspect items.** Anything marked (worked out), (old) or (unsure) on any
   list. These are the ones most likely to be wrong.

## Then

Ask us to settle the contradictions out loud, one at a time. As we do, write
each settled one as a line we can paste into `decisions.md` later, in this
format:

```
### YYYY-MM-DD - [what is true]
- Why: [what we agreed, and what was wrong before]
- Revisit when: [a trigger, or "not planned"]
```

Do not settle any of them yourself. If we cannot agree on one, mark it
`[UNRESOLVED]` and move on.

## At the end

Tell us which single divergence between our two Claudes is most likely to
reach a customer, and why.

---

This sitting exists because each person's Claude has been learning a
different version of the business, privately, for months. Nobody has ever
seen the two side by side. This is usually the most surprising ten minutes
of the whole kit.
