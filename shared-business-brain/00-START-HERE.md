# 00 — Start here

## The shared version

Two versions of this kit exist. This is the one for a business with more than
one person in it. If you work alone, use `solo-operator-brain` — it is
shorter and asks different questions.

The versions differ by how many people are in the business. Not by what
anyone pays for Claude. On a multi-user plan you get one shared brain
outright; on mixed plans one of you can be on Team, one on Pro, one on Free
and you will still all get the same answers, because you will all be reading
the same eight files. The routes are in `03-load-the-brain`.

It also moves with you. Change plans, add people, switch tools — the brain is
text files and it comes along. See `03-load-the-brain/04-moving-between-plans.md`.

## What this fixes

Claude opens every conversation knowing nothing about your business. Worse,
if two of you use it, you each have a *different* Claude: its memory is tied
to one login, so what one person's Claude has learned over months, the
other's has never heard. Neither of you can see the other's.

Two people can ask the same question and get two different answers, and
nobody finds out until a customer notices.

This kit fixes both halves with eight shared files that everyone's Claude
reads before it answers.

About two hours in total, split between you, spread over a week or two.

## One brain, however you hold it

On a multi-user Claude plan, you get a genuinely shared brain: one Project,
shared, holding the eight files. One person uploads, everyone has it, and an
update reaches the whole team at once. Nothing to coordinate.

On mixed or individual plans, the same eight files still give everyone the
same answers — each person loads them their own way. `03-load-the-brain` has
the routes.

Either way, keep the files in **a folder** the business owns. That folder is
what you export, share with a contractor, and carry with you if you change
plans, change accounts, or change AI tools altogether. The brain is text, and
text outlives whatever is holding it this year.

Share those files as widely as suits you: contractors, a new starter, your
accountant, a partner business, someone using a different AI tool entirely.
They are plain text and they work anywhere.
`03-load-the-brain/03-sharing-the-brain.md` suggests which files suit which
audience.

## What is in here

| Folder | What it is | When |

## How the interviews work

One sitting is one file, one chat, one person. Start a new chat, attach the
file named at the top of the sitting, paste the prompt, answer the questions.
At the end Claude writes the finished file. Save it to the shared folder,
close the chat.

Short chats are the point. In a long conversation Claude starts cutting
corners — batching questions, accepting vague answers, eventually writing
answers for you to nod at.

## The sittings

| # | About | Who | Time | Have open |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 00 | What your Claude already knows | Each of you, separately | 10 min | — |
| 01 | Comparing what you each know | Everyone together | 10 min | Your lists from 00 |
| 02 | The business itself | The owner | 12 min | — |
| 03 | What you sell | Whoever knows the details | 15 min | Prices or booking system |
| 04 | Channels, tools and owners | Everyone | 10 min | — |
| 05 | Customer questions | Whoever answers customers | 15 min | Inbox or messages |
| 06 | Pricing and offers | The owner | 12 min | — |
| 07 | Clients and suppliers | The owner | 10 min | — |
| 08 | Decisions already made | Everyone | 10 min | — |
| 09 | How you sound — the rules | The owner | 8 min | — |
| 10 | How you sound — the examples | The owner | 12 min | Your published writing |

Split it between people. Nobody has to do all of it.

## Start with 00 and 01

These two are the reason this version exists, and skipping them is a mistake.

Each of you asks your own Claude what it already knows about the business.
Then you put the lists side by side. What comes out is a picture nobody in
the business has ever seen: where you agree, where your two Claudes have
learned contradictory things, and what is held in exactly one person's head.

Most teams find at least one contradiction that would have reached a
customer. Those get settled out loud and written straight into `decisions.md`.

Anyone using Claude can do sitting 00, on any plan.

## Order

**00, then 01, then 02.** Sitting 02 ends by telling you which later sittings
need adapting for your kind of business. Note it before starting 03.

**09 before 10.** They build the same file in two passes. For sitting 10,
attach the file produced by 09.

The rest can happen in any order, on any day.

## Each sitting, step by step

1. Start a **new chat** — not a continuation of an earlier one.
2. Attach the file named at the top of that sitting, from `02-brain-files`.
3. Paste everything below the line in the prompt.
4. Answer the questions.
5. Copy the finished file out. **Keep the same file name** — the instructions
   and the skills look files up by name.
6. Save it over the blank one, in the shared folder.
7. Close the chat.

If you get interrupted, start a new chat, attach the part-finished file
instead of the blank one, and paste the same prompt.

## Three rules for you

**Do not invent an answer to keep things moving.** Say you do not know, and
it gets marked as an open decision. A made-up answer becomes something the
whole team then works from.

**Push back.** If Claude writes a sentence none of you would say, tell it.

**Where two of you disagree, stop and talk.** That is usually the most useful
ten minutes of the whole exercise, and several sittings are built to provoke
it deliberately.

## When the sittings are done

Open `03-load-the-brain`. Each person picks the route that matches what they
have, and the file owner gets named there.

One thing to watch: if someone already runs a Project with these standing
instructions, do the interviews **outside** it. Those instructions tell
Claude to check past decisions and end by logging one — useful later,
distracting during an interview.

## The thing to remember afterwards

The shared files fix the shared questions. Your private Claude memories keep
running, and keep diverging, whatever anyone is paying. The files are the
only common ground — which is why the decision log matters, and why sitting
01 is worth running again in a year to see how far you have drifted.
