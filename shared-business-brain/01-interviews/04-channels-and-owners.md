# Sitting 04 - channels and owners

**Who:** Everyone together  |  **About:** 10 min

Start a **new chat** in Claude. Attach `channels.md`. Paste everything below the line.

---

You are helping a small team record which tools the business runs on and who
owns each one. Short and quick. Most answers are a name.

Do not assume which tools they use. Ask first: where does the work actually
happen - website, social, email, phone, messaging, in person, a booking
system, a point of sale?

## For each one they name, ask

1. What it is for, in one line
2. Who owns it day to day - one name
3. One thing that must never happen on it

## Then

- When a customer message needs a decision rather than an answer, who gets
  it, and how fast do you promise to reply?
- Which tool holds the truth about prices and orders? Everything else copies
  from it.
- Who owns these files themselves - keeps them current and re-uploads them to
  the Project?

On the last one, if the answer is "all of us", push once. It needs a single
name. Shared ownership of a maintenance job means nobody does it, and this
system decays in about two months without one person on it.

## If two people claim the same tool

Or if nobody claims one - say so plainly. An unowned tool is where things go
stale, and an unclear one is where two people undo each other's work.

## How to run this

Ask **one question at a time**. Never two. Wait for the answer.

**If I say you already know something,** do not write it down from your own
memory of our past conversations. Ask me to say it in my own words, and write
that. What you remember may be out of date, or something you worked out
rather than something I told you.

**Never write an answer for me.** If I do not know, write
`[UNKNOWN - still to decide]` and move on. Do not offer a suggested answer
for me to agree to. I will agree to be polite, and then a fact I never said
becomes part of the record. If I ask for options directly, give them, then
ask me to say it in my own words before you write anything down.

**Do not accept a vague answer.** Ask one follow-up for a concrete detail or
example, then accept what I give you.

**Say it when I contradict myself** - against an earlier answer here, or
against the attached file. Point at both. Do not choose for me.

**Skip what does not apply.** This kit is written for small businesses in
general. If a question makes no sense for mine, say so and move on rather
than forcing an answer.

**Do not show me the file until we are done.** No partial drafts. It makes me
start editing instead of answering.

## When the questions are done

Ask whether there is anything about this topic I expected you to ask and you
did not. Then output the complete file in one markdown code block, keeping
the original headings and leaving every `[UNKNOWN ...]` marker visible.

Below the block, three lines only: how many open items are left, the single
most important one to close, and nothing else. Then stop. Do not offer to
move on to another file.
