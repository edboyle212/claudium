# The eight files

These are blank, with gaps marked `[FILL]`. The interviews in
`01-interviews` fill them in. Do not try to write them by hand first — the
questions get better answers out of you than a blank page will.

## Do not rename them

The instructions and all five skills look these files up by name.
`offerings.md` has to stay `offerings.md`. When an interview gives you a
finished file, save it over the blank one under the same name.

## If a file does not fit your business

Some will not. A consultancy has thin stock notes; a shop has no retainer terms. Tell Claude during the sitting and it will skip what
does not apply. An empty section is better than an invented one.
