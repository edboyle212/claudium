# Answer a customer

## Hard stops - escalate, do not answer

Check the escalation list in customer-faq.md first. If the message matches
anything on it, draft only the holding reply. Do not attempt the substantive
answer.

If the list does not cover it but the message involves health, safety, legal
exposure, or an angry customer threatening to go public, treat it as
escalation anyway and say why.

## Otherwise

1. Answer from customer-faq.md if the question is already there.
2. Take facts from offerings.md only. Never estimate a price or a timing.
3. Match voice.md, but shorter. A reply is not a post.
4. Keep it to three sentences where possible.

## After answering

If the question is not in customer-faq.md and has now come up more than
once, say so and draft the entry to add.
