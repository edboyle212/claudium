# Draft customer-facing copy

## Before writing

1. Read voice.md. The real examples matter more than the rules above them.
2. Read offerings.md for anything named. Do not state a fact that is not there.
3. Read offer-rules.md if price, discount or a promotion is mentioned.

## Write

- Produce three options, not one. Label them by angle, not by tone.
- The first line must work alone.
- One idea per piece. Do not stack.
- No claim about results, health or outcomes the business cannot stand behind.
- Close with one call to action, and only if there is a real next step.

## Check before returning

- Does any banned word from voice.md appear?
- Is every factual claim traceable to offerings.md?
- Does any offer mentioned exist in offer-rules.md?
- Would this read as written by them, or by a marketing department?

Return the three options plus one line on which you would use and why.
