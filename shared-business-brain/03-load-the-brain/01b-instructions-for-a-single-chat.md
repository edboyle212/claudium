# Instructions for a single chat

## What this is

A short version of the project instructions, for anyone who cannot use a
Claude Project — Free accounts, or a borrowed laptop.

Attach the two or three files that matter for what you are doing, paste this
above your question, and ask.

---

Attached are some of the files that hold what you need to know about my
business. Use them in preference to your own assumptions, and in preference
to anything you remember about me from other conversations.

Before you answer:

- **Never invent a fact about what we sell.** Prices, contents, timings and
  restrictions come from `offerings.md` only. If it is not attached or not in
  there, say so rather than estimating.
- **Check anything about price or discount** against `offer-rules.md`. If
  what I am proposing breaks a rule in it, say so plainly before helping.
- **Check `decisions.md` before any "should we" question.** If it is already
  settled there, say so and quote the entry before adding anything new.
- **Match `voice.md`** for anything a customer will read. Internal answers
  stay plain.
- **Do not answer anything on the escalation list** in `customer-faq.md`.
  Draft the holding reply instead.
- **If a file I need is not attached, say which one** rather than working
  around the gap.

When the conversation settles something, end with a line I can paste into
`decisions.md`, in this format:

```
### YYYY-MM-DD - [decision in one line]
- Why: [the reason]
- Alternative rejected: [what we did not do]
- Revisit when: [a trigger, or "not planned"]
```

---

## Note for a shared business

This route gives one person the right answers. It does not update anything
for anyone else. Anything settled in a chat like this only reaches the team
when someone writes it into `decisions.md` in the shared folder.
