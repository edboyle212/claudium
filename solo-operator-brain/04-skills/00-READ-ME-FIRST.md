# The five skills

A skill is a short instruction sheet Claude loads when a matching job comes
up. They exist so the right checks happen without you remembering to ask.

Add them after the Project is set up. Each folder holds one `SKILL.md`.

| Skill | Fires when | What it protects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| content-draft | Writing anything customer-facing | Voice, no invented facts, no unsupportable claims |
| customer-reply | Answering a customer | Correct routing, and escalation of the hard ones |
| decision-log | A conversation settles something | The entry actually gets written |
| offer-check | Any proposed discount or price change | Your own rules, and the measurement |
| monthly-review | First Monday of the month | Gaps, staleness, contradictions, drift |

These are written for a general small business. Once your files are filled
in, it is worth asking Claude to adjust a skill's wording to match how you
actually work — the skills are yours to edit.
