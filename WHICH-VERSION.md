# Which version do I use?

Two kits. The difference is **how many people work in the business**, not
what any of you pay for Claude.

## `solo-operator-brain`

You run it on your own, or with help that does not use Claude.

Ten sittings, about ninety minutes, all done by you. One extra file,
`how-i-work.md`, which doubles as the handover document for the first person
you hire or the fortnight you are ill.

## `shared-business-brain`

Two or more of you work in the business and use Claude.

Eleven sittings, about two hours, split between you. It has one sitting the
solo version does not: everyone asks their own Claude what it already knows,
then you compare the lists. Each person's Claude has been learning a
different version of the business, privately, for months, and nobody has ever
seen those side by side. It is usually the most surprising part of the kit,
and it produces real decisions to write down.

## What about our Claude plans?

They do not decide which version you use, and they do not have to match.

A shared brain is a **folder of eight files** that everyone works from. How
each person loads those files into Claude depends on what they happen to
have, and the routes are in `03-load-the-brain`. One of you can be on Team,
one on Pro, one on Free, and you all get the same answers — because you are
all reading the same files.

On a multi-user plan you get one genuinely shared brain — one Project, one
upload, everyone on it.

Share the files with anyone you like — staff, contractors, a new starter,
your accountant. They are plain text and they work anywhere, including with
other AI tools. See `03-sharing-the-brain.md` for which files suit which
audience, and `04-moving-between-plans.md` for carrying the brain between
plans, accounts or tools.

## Not sure yet

Start with `solo-operator-brain`. The eight files transfer unchanged. When
someone else joins, take the comparison sitting from the shared version and
run it as a one-off.
