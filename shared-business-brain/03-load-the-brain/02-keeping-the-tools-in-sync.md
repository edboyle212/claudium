# Keeping the tools in sync

## What this is

Not software. It is the written agreement that stops the same question
getting two different answers depending on who a customer asks, or where.

However each of you loads the files, the other surfaces - your website,
booking system, autoresponders - are not part of it. Nor is the folder
itself. This document covers those.

## Direction rules

1. **Wherever prices really live is upstream.** Change them there first, then
   copy into offerings.md. Never the reverse.
2. **The shared folder holds the master files.** The Project holds a copy.
3. **Autoresponders and chatbots are downstream and read-only.** Their
   answers are pasted from customer-faq.md. Nobody writes a new answer
   directly into a flow - if they do, the bot and the people drift apart, and
   the customer sees it.
4. **Claude drafts, a person pastes.** It never edits the master files.

## The sync ritual

Attach it to something that already happens, or it will not happen.

| When | What | Owner | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| A decision is made | Paste the drafted entry into decisions.md | whoever was in the chat | 2 |
| A price or offering changes | Update offerings.md, then any automation | [FILL] | 10 |
| The same question arrives three times | Add it to customer-faq.md, then to any automation | [FILL] | 10 |
| First Monday of the month | Run the monthly review, re-load changed files | [FILL] | 15 |

## Re-loading after a change

Nothing updates itself from the folder. When a file changes, the owner says
so, and everyone re-loads it the way they load it: a shared Project is
re-uploaded once for everybody; a personal Project is re-uploaded by its
owner; anyone attaching files per chat just picks up the new copy next time.

## The limit that matters most

**Each person's Claude memory is separate, and it is still running.** On
every plan, and whatever route you use, what one person tells Claude in a
private chat stays invisible to everyone else - and those private memories keep diverging every
month, quietly, exactly as they did before you built this.

The shared files are the only common ground. That is why decisions.md exists,
and it is why sitting 01 is worth repeating once a year: run the comparison
again and see how far the two versions have drifted.
