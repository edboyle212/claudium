# Sitting 00 - what claude already knows

**Who:** Each person, separately  |  **About:** 10 min

Start a **new chat** in Claude. Attach nothing. Paste everything below the line.

---

I have been using you for a while to work on the business I am part of. We
are about to build a shared set of files for the whole team, and I want to
see what you are already holding first.

## What to do

Go through what you know about this business - what I have asked you to
remember, and, if you can search our past conversations, what comes up there
too. List it back to me.

Group it under these headings, and skip any you have nothing for:

- What the business is and who it serves
- What we sell, and what it costs
- Customers, clients, partners and suppliers
- How we sound - voice, tone, words we like or avoid
- Pricing, offers and discounts
- Tools we use and how we work
- Decisions that have been made
- Anything else

## How to write it

One short line per item. Plain facts, no commentary. Mark each line:

- **(told)** - I said this to you directly
- **(worked out)** - you inferred it rather than being told
- **(old)** - it came up more than about three months ago
- **(unsure)** - you cannot tell which

Guessing at the source is worse than admitting you cannot tell.

## What not to do

Do not fill gaps. If you know six things, list six. Do not tidy up
contradictions - if two things you remember disagree, list both and say so.
Do not write it as a summary. A flat list is what I want.

## At the end

Tell me which two or three items you are least confident about, and why.

---

**Everyone runs this separately, in their own account.** Claude's memory is
tied to one login. What one person's Claude knows, another's has never heard,
and neither of you can see the other's. Save your list. Sitting 00b compares
them, and the differences are the point.
